📚 Statutory Framework & Judicial Precedents
Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA) 2023 Sec 61 - 63
Governs primary and secondary admissibility of electronic records. Mandates cryptographic verification and certified statements for digital evidence admissibility in Indian courts.
Arjun Panditrao Khotkar v. K. K. Gorantyal (2020) 7 SCC 1
Hon'ble Supreme Court of India 3-Judge Bench ruling making Section 65B(4) electronic certificates mandatory as a condition precedent for digital evidence admissibility.
Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS) 2023 Sec 173 & 193
Mandates compulsory audio-video recording of search and seizure operations and time-bound digital transmission of investigation charge sheets to trial magistrates.
DPDP Act 2023 & IT Act 2000 Privacy & Sec 4
Digital Personal Data Protection Act safeguards citizen confidentiality and witness privacy; IT Act 2000 Sec 4 legally equates digital records with physical paperwork.
NIST SP 800-88 & ISO/IEC 27001 Global Standards
NIST standard for digital evidence sanitization, cryptographic checksum preservation, and ISO 27001 information security controls.
